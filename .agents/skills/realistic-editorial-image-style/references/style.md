# Realistic Editorial Runtime Notes

- Use natural-language scene descriptions rather than keyword piles.
- For photographic styles, include camera/lens, directional light, material texture, and small imperfections.
- Useful cues: natural window light, warm side light, shallow depth of field, visible fabric weave, gentle wrinkles, soft shadows, film grain, slight off-center composition.
- Avoid: perfect, flawless, symmetrical, pristine, ultra-HD rendering, CGI, app screenshots, login dialogs, source URLs, usernames, research notes, and tool status cards.
- When people appear, they should look alert and natural: bright eyes, lively expression, relaxed upright posture. Avoid tired, sleepy, drooping, or slumped body language unless explicitly requested.
- For product and outfit images, keep the subject materially specific and visually grounded instead of abstract or generic.
