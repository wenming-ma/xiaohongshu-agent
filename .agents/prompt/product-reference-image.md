# Product Reference Image Alignment

Use this when the user provides or mentions product reference images, skincare,
beauty products, packaging, shelf-style presentation, or product comparison.

- 中文触发词：产品参考图、参考图对齐、护肤、敏感肌、精华、货架感、弱化品牌名、包装对比
- reference image alignment: keep product category, silhouette, material cues, and package hierarchy consistent
- clean shelf or vanity composition, soft shadow, controlled reflections
- weaken brand names and avoid oversized logos unless explicitly requested
- show realistic product scale and avoid hallucinated app UI, login dialogs, diagnostic cards, or unrelated screenshots
- suitable for skincare, makeup, fragrance, supplements, and small lifestyle products
