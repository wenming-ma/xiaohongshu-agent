# Glass Acrylic Refraction

## Use When
Use for transparent, translucent, crystal, glass, acrylic, glossy packaging, or
refraction-focused product visuals.

## Constraints
- Keep refraction and reflection physically plausible.
- Do not obscure the product identity behind excessive glow or rainbow effects.
- Avoid fake text on transparent packaging unless exact copy is provided.

## Prompt Template
Generate a refined glass or acrylic material image of `{subject}` with
transparent edges, subtle refraction, clean specular highlights, soft caustic
shadows, and a controlled studio background. The material should feel premium,
not plastic or over-filtered.
