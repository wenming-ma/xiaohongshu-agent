# Ceramic Wood Stone Tabletop

## Use When
Use for food, home, skincare, craft, coffee, tea, fragrance, or product images
that need natural surface richness.

## Constraints
- Choose one dominant surface material, not a cluttered mix.
- Keep surface texture supportive rather than louder than the subject.
- Do not add irrelevant lifestyle props outside the current group.

## Prompt Template
Generate a tabletop editorial image of `{subject}` on `{surface_material}` with
natural texture, grounded shadows, gentle highlights, and a calm premium color
palette. The surface should make the subject feel tactile and real while keeping
the scene minimal.
