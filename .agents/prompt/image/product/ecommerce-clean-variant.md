# Ecommerce Clean Variant

## Use When

Use for clean product catalog-like images that still feel premium and social
media friendly.

## Constraints

- Clean does not mean sterile or empty.
- Keep one product family per image.
- Avoid generated text overlays.
- Preserve material and packaging details.

## Prompt Template

Create a clean ecommerce-style product image for `{product_subject}`.

Include:
- Background: `{background_style}`.
- Product angle: `{product_angle}`.
- Supporting props: `{supporting_props}`.
- Shadow: natural contact shadow.
- No fake logos, fake labels, or app UI.
