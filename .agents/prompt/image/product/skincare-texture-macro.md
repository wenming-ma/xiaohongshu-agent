# Skincare Texture Macro

## Use When

Use for skincare, makeup, cream, serum, balm, or product texture detail images.

## Constraints

- Texture should look touchable and realistic.
- Do not make medical or efficacy claims visually.
- Avoid fake ingredient text and unreadable labels.
- Keep product packaging consistent with references when provided.

## Prompt Template

Create a skincare texture macro for `{product_subject}`.

Include:
- Texture: `{texture_description}`.
- Packaging nearby: `{packaging_detail}`.
- Surface: `{surface_style}`.
- Lighting: clean soft highlights.
- No fake label text, UI, or clinical claims.
