# Ingredient Still Life

## Use When

Use for skincare, food, supplement, or wellness products where ingredients or
materials support the story.

## Constraints

- Ingredients must be plausible and relevant.
- Do not imply unsupported efficacy.
- Keep ingredients secondary to product or topic.
- Avoid medical visuals unless explicitly requested.

## Prompt Template

Create an ingredient still life for `{product_or_topic}`.

Show:
- Main subject: `{main_subject}`.
- Supporting ingredients: `{ingredient_list}`.
- Surface: `{surface_style}`.
- Lighting: `{lighting_style}`.
- Mood: clean, credible, editorial.
