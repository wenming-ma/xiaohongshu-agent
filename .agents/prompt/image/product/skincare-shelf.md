# Skincare Clean Shelf

## Use When
Use for skincare, beauty, fragrance, bathroom shelf, product comparison, or
routine recommendation image groups.

## Constraints
- Keep packaging clean and plausible; avoid unreadable fake ingredient blocks.
- De-emphasize brands unless required.
- Avoid medical claims in the image.

## Prompt Template
Create a 3:4 vertical skincare shelf editorial. Place the current product group
on a clean bathroom or vanity shelf with soft diffused light, glass or ceramic
textures, subtle water highlights, and calm negative space. The result should
feel premium, realistic, and review-ready.
