# Hero Subject Negative Space

## Use When

Use for cover images or product hero shots where one subject should dominate
the frame.

## Constraints

- One clear hero subject.
- Negative space should support title overlay if needed, but do not generate
  fake text unless explicitly requested.
- Avoid busy backgrounds.
- Keep subject edges and silhouette readable.

## Prompt Template

Create a vertical hero image for `{image_subject}`.

Composition:
- Hero subject placed slightly above center.
- Controlled negative space around the subject.
- Background color or texture: `{background_style}`.
- Detail level: `{detail_priority}`.
- No unrelated props, UI, or diagnostic cards.
