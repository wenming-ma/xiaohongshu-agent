# Detail Crop Texture

## Use When

Use for detail images that show fabric, packaging, food texture, skincare
texture, or material quality.

## Constraints

- Crop should clarify a detail, not hide missing content.
- Keep texture natural rather than hyper-sharpened plastic.
- Avoid unreadable fake labels or text.
- Match the parent image style.

## Prompt Template

Create a close detail crop for `{detail_subject}`.

Focus on:
- Texture or material: `{texture_details}`.
- Scale cue: `{scale_reference}`.
- Lighting: `{lighting_style}`.
- Style continuity: `{style_context}`.
- No fake text, UI, or unrelated decorative icons.
