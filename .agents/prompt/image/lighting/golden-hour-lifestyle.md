# Golden Hour Lifestyle

## Use When

Use for outdoor travel, citywalk, camping, fitness, or lifestyle images that
need warmth and mood.

## Constraints

- Keep golden light believable and scene-specific.
- Avoid fantasy glow unless requested.
- Ensure the subject remains readable.
- Do not introduce unrelated people or landmarks.

## Prompt Template

Create a golden-hour lifestyle image for `{scene_subject}`.

Include:
- Time feeling: late afternoon or early evening.
- Light: warm edge light and soft shadows.
- Environment: `{scene_environment}`.
- Key objects: `{visual_anchors}`.
- Style: `{style_context}`.
