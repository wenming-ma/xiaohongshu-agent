# Hard Flash Editorial

## Use When

Use for bolder fashion, nightlife, product, or cafe images that need a sharper
magazine-like flash look.

## Constraints

- Use flash intentionally; do not make everything harsh.
- Preserve product or outfit color accuracy.
- Avoid red-eye, plastic skin, or messy glare.
- Keep composition clean for mobile viewing.

## Prompt Template

Create a hard-flash editorial image for `{image_subject}`.

Use:
- Direct flash with crisp shadows.
- Slightly raw camera feel.
- Controlled background: `{background_style}`.
- Strong subject separation.
- No fake UI, login panels, or random text.
