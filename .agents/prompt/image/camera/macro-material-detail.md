# Macro Material Detail

## Use When
Use for detail images where texture, fabric, finish, ingredient, product surface,
or craftsmanship is more important than showing the full scene.

## Constraints
- Focus on one clear material detail from the current group.
- Use shallow depth of field without making the object unrecognizable.
- Avoid unrelated decorative props that compete with the detail.

## Prompt Template
Generate a close macro detail image of `{material_or_detail}` from
`{group_subject}`. Use tight framing, tactile texture, controlled highlights,
natural imperfections, and soft background falloff so the viewer can immediately
understand the material quality and why this detail matters.
