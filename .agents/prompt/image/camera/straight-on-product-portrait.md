# Straight-On Product Portrait

## Use When
Use when a product, object, cover asset, or outfit board needs a clear front
view with minimal perspective distortion.

## Constraints
- Keep the subject centered and readable on mobile.
- Preserve product proportions and recognizable structure.
- Do not tilt the object into dramatic perspective unless requested.

## Prompt Template
Generate a straight-on 3:4 product portrait of `{subject}` against
`{background}`. Use clean front-facing alignment, accurate proportions, balanced
studio light, soft grounding shadow, and enough detail for the subject to feel
real instead of like a flat icon.
