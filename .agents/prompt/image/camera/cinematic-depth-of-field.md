# Cinematic Depth Of Field

## Use When
Use for lifestyle, travel, food, product, or fashion images that should feel
photographic and atmospheric rather than diagrammatic.

## Constraints
- Keep the main subject sharp and unambiguous.
- Blur only background context, not key objects required by the group.
- Avoid cinematic effects that make the image dark, muddy, or off-topic.

## Prompt Template
Generate a cinematic editorial photograph of `{subject}` in `{scene}` with a
sharp focal plane, natural lens compression, soft background bokeh, realistic
ambient detail, and a warm but controlled color grade. The image should feel
captured by a real camera, not assembled from stock icons.
