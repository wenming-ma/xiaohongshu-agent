# Low-Angle Hero Scale

## Use When
Use when the subject should feel bold, premium, powerful, or larger-than-life
while still remaining realistic.

## Constraints
- Do not exaggerate scale so much that the product or object becomes implausible.
- Keep key features visible despite the lower camera angle.
- Avoid adding people unless explicitly requested.

## Prompt Template
Generate a low-angle hero image of `{subject}` with controlled perspective,
clean foreground, subtle environmental scale cues, and premium lighting. The
subject should feel confident and substantial while staying physically plausible
and directly tied to the current content group.
