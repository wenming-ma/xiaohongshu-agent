# Cafe Dessert Warm Film

## Use When
Use for dessert shops, cafe routes, city weekend treats, cake, coffee, tea, or
bakery scenes that need a warm film look.

## Constraints
- Keep the mood cozy but not yellow-tinted or blurry.
- Do not add unrelated clothing, cosmetics, login dialogs, or research cards.
- If text appears, it must be minimal and in simplified Chinese.

## Prompt Template
Create a 3:4 vertical warm-film cafe dessert image. Show the selected dessert
and drink on a small table with ceramic plates, soft window light, gentle film
grain, and realistic shadows. Preserve appetizing detail and quiet city-weekend
atmosphere.
