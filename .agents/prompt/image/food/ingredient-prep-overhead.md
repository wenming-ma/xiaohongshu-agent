# Ingredient Prep Overhead

## Use When

Use for cooking, recipe, cafe, or food education images with ingredients laid
out clearly.

## Constraints

- Keep ingredients recognizable and relevant.
- Avoid messy countertops.
- Do not add unrelated fashion or product props.
- Food should look fresh and natural.

## Prompt Template

Create an overhead ingredient prep image for `{food_topic}`.

Include:
- Ingredients: `{ingredients}`.
- Tools or dishes: `{prep_tools}`.
- Surface: `{surface_style}`.
- Lighting: natural or studio-soft.
- No fake menu text, UI, or unrelated objects.
