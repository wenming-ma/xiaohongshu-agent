# Food Editorial Tabletop

## Use When
Use for restaurant, dessert, cafe, brunch, home cooking, or snack recommendation
image groups.

## Constraints
- Use food from the current group only.
- Avoid menu screenshots, QR-code ordering pages, app interface elements, and
  unrelated lifestyle diagrams.
- Keep steam, crumbs, tableware, and lighting realistic.

## Prompt Template
Generate a warm 3:4 vertical food editorial photograph on a tabletop. Arrange
the current dishes and drinks with natural light, real texture, appetizing color,
and small contextual props. The composition should feel photographed in a real
cafe or dining setting, not rendered as a poster.
