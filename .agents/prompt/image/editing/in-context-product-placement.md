# In-Context Product Placement

## Use When
Use when a product or object should be shown naturally in a real usage context
instead of as a studio packshot.

## Constraints
- The product remains the main subject and must not be hidden by props.
- The context must be relevant to the current group, not a random lifestyle
  backdrop.
- Avoid hands or people unless requested or necessary for scale.

## Prompt Template
Generate a realistic in-context editorial image of `{product}` in
`{usage_scene}`. Keep the product clear, correctly scaled, and naturally placed
with believable lighting, contact shadows, and supporting details that explain
how it is used.
