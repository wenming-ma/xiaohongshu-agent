# Product Benefit Scene

## Use When
Use when a product or item should visually communicate one benefit or use case
without a text-heavy infographic.

## Constraints
- Show one benefit clearly; do not cram multiple unrelated claims into one
  image.
- The benefit must be visually plausible from the current research or user
  request.
- Avoid fake charts, badges, and before-after medical claims.

## Prompt Template
Generate a realistic benefit-led scene for `{product_or_item}` showing
`{benefit}` through placement, lighting, supporting objects, and composition.
Keep the image photographic, focused, and suitable for Feishu review before
publication planning.
