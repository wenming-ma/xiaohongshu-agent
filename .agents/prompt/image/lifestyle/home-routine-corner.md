# Home Routine Corner

## Use When

Use for home routine, wellness, study, skincare, reading, or morning/evening
lifestyle scenes.

## Constraints

- Make the scene specific, not showroom-generic.
- Keep privacy-safe details.
- Avoid messy clutter and fake notes.
- Do not add people unless requested.

## Prompt Template

Create a home routine corner for `{routine_topic}`.

Include:
- Main activity objects: `{visual_anchors}`.
- Corner setting: `{home_setting}`.
- Light: `{lighting_style}`.
- Mood: calm, lived-in, editorial.
- No app screens, login UI, or fake text notes.
