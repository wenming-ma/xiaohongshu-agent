# Camping Tabletop Atmosphere

## Use When
Use for camping, picnic, outdoor table setup, gear layout, coffee outdoors, or
camp meal image groups.

## Constraints
- Keep the setup practical and not overcrowded.
- Do not mix unrelated city interiors, login windows, or abstract arrows.
- Respect weather, season, and audience constraints from the request.

## Prompt Template
Create a 3:4 vertical camping tabletop editorial. Arrange the current camping
items or meal components on a portable table with natural outdoor light, canvas,
wood, stainless steel, and muted earth tones. The scene should feel calm,
usable, and photographed outdoors.
