# Citywalk Editorial Scene

## Use When
Use for city walk, local travel, weekend routes, museum, bookstore, cafe street,
or neighborhood exploration image groups.

## Constraints
- Show the scene or objects implied by the current group, not generic landmarks.
- Avoid map screenshots, app UI, and fake navigation panels.
- Keep people optional and secondary unless requested.

## Prompt Template
Generate a 3:4 vertical citywalk editorial image. Use real-world street texture,
soft daylight, layered storefront or sidewalk details, and a clear focal point
from the current group. Make it feel like a natural Xiaohongshu travel photo.
