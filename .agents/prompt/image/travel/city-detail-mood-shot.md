# City Detail Mood Shot

## Use When

Use for citywalk, neighborhood, cafe street, or travel mood images that need
environmental detail.

## Constraints

- Show place mood through textures, light, and objects.
- Avoid fake landmark claims.
- Do not include readable storefront text unless verified.
- Keep scene useful for the current post angle.

## Prompt Template

Create a city detail mood shot for `{city_scene}`.

Include:
- Detail focus: `{detail_anchor}`.
- Environment: `{environment_context}`.
- Lighting: `{lighting_style}`.
- Mood: `{style_context}`.
- No fake signs, UI, or unrelated crowds.
