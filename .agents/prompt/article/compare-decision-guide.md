# Compare Decision Guide

## Use When

Use for articles that help readers choose between options.

## Constraints

- Compare decision criteria, not just features.
- State who each option is for.
- Use evidence refs for factual claims.
- Include a quick summary for readers who skim.

## Prompt Template

Create a comparison article for `{topic}`.

Include:
- Decision summary.
- Criteria table.
- Option-by-option analysis.
- Who should choose what.
- Final caveats and evidence notes.
