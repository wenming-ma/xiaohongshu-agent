# Deep Explainer Article Outline

## Use When
Use for long-form article routes, trend explainers, concept education, or
structured deep dives.

## Constraints
- Keep every section traceable to research outputs.
- Avoid unsupported claims and invented citations.
- Preserve the user's audience and reading difficulty level.

## Prompt Template
Create a long-form article outline with a clear thesis, 4-6 sections, evidence
slots, practical takeaways, and a final summary. Each section should state what
the reader learns and which research evidence supports it.
