# Source Backed Summary

## Use When
Use when turning research findings into a concise article summary or briefing.

## Constraints
- Do not flatten uncertainty; call out weak evidence.
- Keep source-derived points separate from interpretation.
- Avoid referencing unavailable internal browser state.

## Prompt Template
Summarize the research into source-backed points. For each point, include the
claim, supporting evidence, confidence level, and how it should shape the final
content. End with open risks or gaps.
