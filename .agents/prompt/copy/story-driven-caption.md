# Story-Driven Caption

## Use When

Use when a post should feel like lived experience rather than a list of tips.

## Constraints

- Keep story compact and relevant to the promise.
- Do not invent personal identity details.
- End with useful takeaways.
- Match the generated visual sequence.

## Prompt Template

Write a story-driven caption for `{topic}`.

Structure:
- Opening moment.
- Friction or realization.
- What changed.
- Practical takeaways.
- Gentle question for Feishu review or final audience.
