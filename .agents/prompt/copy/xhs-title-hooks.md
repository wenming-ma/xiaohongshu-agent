# Xiaohongshu Title Hooks

## Use When
Use when drafting or revising titles for image, article, or video deliveries.

## Constraints
- Avoid fake certainty, exaggerated claims, and clickbait that content cannot
  support.
- Preserve the user's theme, audience, and style constraints.
- Keep titles natural for Xiaohongshu, not SEO-stuffed.

## Prompt Template
Draft 8 Xiaohongshu-style titles for the current content. Mix practical benefit,
scene specificity, contrast, and curiosity. Keep each title concise, concrete,
and aligned with the actual research and final media.
