# Product Review Copy

## Use When
Use for product recommendation, comparison, beauty, gear, appliance, clothing,
or accessory review posts.

## Constraints
- Separate observation from claim; avoid unsupported efficacy guarantees.
- Keep brand mentions controlled and privacy-safe.
- Include fit, use case, tradeoff, and who should skip it when relevant.

## Prompt Template
Write product review copy with sections for scene, key advantage, usage detail,
tradeoff, and audience fit. Keep the tone honest and concrete, with no medical,
financial, or absolute performance claims unless backed by supplied evidence.
