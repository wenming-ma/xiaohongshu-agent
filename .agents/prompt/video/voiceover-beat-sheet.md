# Voiceover Beat Sheet

## Use When

Use when a video route needs a spoken script that is paced and easy to review.

## Constraints

- Keep sentences short and speakable.
- Avoid dense article phrasing.
- Make each beat visually supportable.
- Preserve evidence-backed claims.

## Prompt Template

Write a voiceover beat sheet for `{video_plan}`.

Return:
- Beat title.
- Spoken line.
- Visual support.
- Timing estimate.
- Risk or claim note.
