# Short Video Hook Script

## Use When
Use for short video routes that need a hook, sequence, narration, or editing
rhythm.

## Constraints
- Keep the hook truthful and supported by the content.
- Avoid copyrighted lyrics, imitation of named creators, and unsafe stunts.
- Match the requested platform mood and audience.

## Prompt Template
Write a short video script with a 3-second hook, scene beats, narration lines,
on-screen text, and pacing notes. Keep each beat visually actionable and tied to
the current research or downloaded material.
