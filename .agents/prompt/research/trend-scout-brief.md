# Trend Scout Brief

## Use When

Use when the Research Agent needs to scan a topic space and turn weak signals
into candidate Xiaohongshu angles.

## Constraints

- Separate observed signals from interpretation.
- Prefer concrete audience situations over generic trend words.
- Keep evidence refs attached to each candidate angle.
- Do not invent popularity metrics.

## Prompt Template

Create a trend scout brief for `{topic}` and `{audience}`.

Return:
- Current signals: short bullets with source refs.
- Audience tension: what problem, desire, or hesitation makes this useful.
- Candidate angles: 3 to 6 post directions.
- Evidence gaps: what still needs validation.
- Recommended next search query list.
