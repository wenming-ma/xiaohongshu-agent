# Audience Painpoint Mining

## Use When

Use when the Research Agent needs user-centered hooks instead of broad topic
summaries.

## Constraints

- Mine emotional friction, decision friction, and practical friction.
- Tie each painpoint to a scene or moment.
- Avoid demographic stereotypes.
- Do not overfit to one comment or one creator.

## Prompt Template

Analyze `{topic}` for `{audience}` and produce:
- Painpoints: 5 concrete pains with scene context.
- Desired outcomes: what the audience wants to feel or achieve.
- Content promises: safe promises the post can make.
- Hooks to avoid: claims that are too broad, fake, or overconfident.
