# Food Editorial Tabletop

Use this for food, dessert, brunch, cafe, or restaurant image posts that need a
warm editorial tabletop visual direction.

- 中文触发词：甜品、探店、咖啡、蛋糕、桌面美食摄影、自然光、温暖胶片感、餐桌构图
- food editorial photography, warm film color, natural window light
- tabletop composition with visible utensils, cup, plate, napkin, and subtle crumbs
- make the hero food clear: cake, coffee, pastry, noodles, or the named dish
- avoid sterile stock-photo lighting and avoid turning the post into a menu board
- leave tasteful negative space for short Chinese title text if needed
