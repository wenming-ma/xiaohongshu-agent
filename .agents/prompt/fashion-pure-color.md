# Fashion Pure Color Single Look

Use this for outfit image posts where the user asks for pure color backgrounds,
flat lay, one outfit per image, no people, no model, or single-look styling.

- 中文触发词：纯色背景、平铺、单套穿搭、不要人物、无模特、衣服组合、穿搭详情图
- pure color background, clean solid backdrop, no decorative room scene
- one complete outfit per image: top, bottom, shoes, bag or small accessories
- flat lay or tidy product arrangement, no person, no mannequin, no body parts
- preserve fabric texture and realistic clothing scale
